** Being At Home

*** Some Config Ideas

Make your config OS reactive

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
  ((eq system-type 'android)
  (message (format "Loading Android Config: %s" system-type))
  (greg/android-setup-items))
#+END_SRC

Put your toolbar at the bottom.

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
(menu-bar-set-tool-bar-position `bottom)
#+END_SRC

Bind your volume buttons

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
(menu-bar-set-tool-bar-position `bottom)
#+END_SRC

Configure your menu

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
(tool-bar-add-item item)
#+END_SRC
